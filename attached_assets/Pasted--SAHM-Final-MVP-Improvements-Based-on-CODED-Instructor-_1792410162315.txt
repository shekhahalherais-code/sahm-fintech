## PHASE 1 — Simplified Financial Dashboard

After the user uploads a PDF financial report, display a clean, beginner-friendly results page.

Show 3–5 key financial metrics using elegant cards:

- Revenue / Operating Income (use the correct term from the report)
- Net Profit
- Total Assets
- Total Liabilities or Debt, depending on the company's financial statements
- Total Equity

Each card should display:

- Financial metric name
- Actual extracted value
- Correct currency and reporting unit
- Source page number
- A short, simple explanation
- Year-over-year percentage change, only when verified comparable data is available

Example:

Net Profit: 120.4 million KWD
+12% compared with the previous year

This is only a UI example. Never use these sample numbers as actual financial data.

Use the existing SAHM brand colors and Google Stitch layout.

Avoid unnecessary tables, repeated information, and overly long sections.

## PHASE 2 — AI-Powered Educational Explanations

Improve the existing simplified explanation feature.

For every financial metric, show two short sections:

**1. What does this number mean?**

Explain the financial term in beginner-friendly language.

**2. What does this mean for a beginner investor?**

Explain how an investor could interpret the number and what other information should be considered.

The second section must provide genuine educational insight rather than repeat the financial value, reporting date, or source page.

For example:

"A rise in net profit can be a positive sign because the company earned more after expenses. However, investors should also compare revenue, expenses, and previous years before drawing conclusions."

Requirements:

- Use the existing AI integration.
- Ground explanations in the uploaded report.
- Support Arabic and English.
- Keep explanations concise, preferably 1–3 sentences per section.
- Avoid complicated financial terminology unless explained.
- Do not invent company performance conclusions.
- Do not provide buy, sell, or hold recommendations.
- Save generated explanations in MongoDB.
- Reuse saved explanations to avoid repeated AI requests.
- Display the source reference separately from the educational interpretation.

## PHASE 3 — Green Flags and Red Flags

Add a small section titled:

Arabic: "مؤشرات تستحق الانتباه"
English: "Financial Signals to Watch"

Display:

- Green indicators for supported positive financial developments
- Red or amber indicators for supported financial risks or warning signs
- Neutral indicators when the information is inconclusive

Possible examples:

- Net profit growth
- Revenue or operating income growth
- Declining profit margins
- Rising liabilities relative to assets or equity
- Increasing costs
- Inconsistent earnings

IMPORTANT:

- Financial indicators must be based on actual verified report data.
- Do not assume that growth automatically means a company is financially healthy.
- Do not treat every increase in liabilities as a negative sign, especially for banks.
- Use sector-aware interpretation when possible.
- Only calculate percentages from comparable reporting periods.
- If previous-year information is missing, display "Insufficient data for comparison."
- Do not fabricate positive or negative signals.
- Include a brief explanation of why each signal matters.
- Never present these indicators as investment recommendations.

Keep this section visually minimal, with no more than 3–4 meaningful signals.

- What does net profit mean?

Requirements:

8. Do not invent figures, sources, or financial conclusions.
9. Do not give personalized investment advice.
10. Keep the chat compact and visually consistent with the current SAHM design.

Implement the chat as a separate component without interfering with the existing report analysis workflow.
