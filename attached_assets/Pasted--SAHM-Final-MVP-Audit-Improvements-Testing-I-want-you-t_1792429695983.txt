## PHASE 3 — YEAR-OVER-YEAR COMPARISONS

If verified current-year and previous-year figures are available, calculate the percentage change.

Formula:

Percentage Change = ((Current Year - Previous Year) / Previous Year) × 100

Handle zero, negative, and missing previous-year values appropriately. Do not show a percentage when the comparison is not meaningful.

Display a simple explanation such as:

"Net profit increased by 12% compared with the previous year."

Only show this statement when the underlying figures support it.

Never generate arbitrary growth percentages.

Preserve existing charts and visual components.

## PHASE 4 — GREEN FLAGS & RED FLAGS

Check whether this feature already exists.

Use the title:

Arabic: "مؤشرات مالية تستحق الانتباه"

English: "Financial Signals to Watch"

Possible positive signals:
- Increasing profitability.
- Improving profit margins.
- Strengthening equity.
- Improving operating cash flow.

Possible warning signals:
- Declining profitability.
- Increasing debt relative to relevant financial measures.
- Weak operating cash flow.
- Significant deterioration in financial performance.

Requirements:

- Every signal must be based on verified financial data.
- Include a short explanation of why the signal matters.

- Consider the company's industry when interpreting metrics.
- Do not treat a single metric as proof of financial health.
- Do not automatically classify a company as a good or bad investment.
- If insufficient data is available, clearly state that the signal cannot be assessed.
- Never fabricate signals to fill empty cards.

Use subtle green, amber, and red visual indicators consistent with SAHM's existing brand colors.

Do not create an investment recommendation or buy/sell rating.
